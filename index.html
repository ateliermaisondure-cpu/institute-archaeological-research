Institute of Archaeological Research

A research centre dedicated to the study, preservation and interpretation of the archaeological heritage of Kazakhstan and the wider Eurasian region.

About the Institute Explore our structure

About us

Archaeology within the academic environment of Margulan University

The Institute brings together archaeological research, fieldwork, conservation practice, collections management and interdisciplinary study. Its activities connect university scholarship with the documentation and responsible preservation of cultural heritage.

Research Programme

IRN BR28712263 “Development of an Integrated Research and Innovation System for Training Teachers to Support Gifted Children in Kazakhstan”

The programme is implemented by Margulan University as part of a state-funded research initiative. The Institute of Archaeological Research contributes to the project as a structural unit of the University, supporting its research, educational and interdisciplinary activities.

Principal activities

Research directions

01

Archaeological Research

Field investigation, documentation and interpretation of archaeological sites and cultural landscapes.

02

Conservation and Restoration

Scientific examination, stabilisation and preservation of archaeological finds and other heritage materials.

03

Collections and Documentation

Registration, storage, study and gradual digital documentation of archaeological collections.

04

Student Education and Training

Academic instruction, field practice and professional training for students in archaeology, conservation, heritage documentation and related disciplines.

Structure

The Institute unites specialised units working in archaeological research, fieldwork, conservation, education, experimental archaeology and bioarchaeology.
